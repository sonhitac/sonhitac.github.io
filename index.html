What this site is

I'm applying for the Assistant Professor, Biomedical Communications position at the University of Toronto Mississauga. This website is a companion to my formal application materials that highlights how I think, what I make, and how I teach, before going to the full cover letter, CV, and statements.

Across the three threads of research, teaching, and visual practice, my work asks the same question: how does a visual or written simplification of biology shape who can understand it, and who gets left out when that simplification isn't examined? Complex biological material don't fail to be understood because it is complicated; it fails because it hasn't yet been drawn, structured, or sequenced appropriately.

I hold a PhD in Molecular and Cell Biology from University of Toronto (2021) and currently work as a Strategic Grant Writer at University of Toronto.
