Exhibition, 2023

Deconstructing Biology

A solo exhibition at the Toronto Public Library that used bold abstraction to make molecular-level biological processes approachable and playful for viewers with no scientific training.

The ten-piece digital art series extended my illustration practice into the community where I grew up, treating public accessibility as the exhibition's central design problem rather than an afterthought. It ran as a solo show at the Toronto Public Library in 2023.
